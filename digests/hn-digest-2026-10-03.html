From the creator of Redis; run LLM locally with ds4

来自 Redis 创作者：用 ds4 本地运行大语言模型

▲ 191 分 · fibo · 56 评论

ds4 是 Redis 作者 antirez 开源的 C 语言本地推理引擎，采用非对称 2-bit 量化压缩 MoE 专家、保持关键共享路径高精度，使 DeepSeek V4/V4.1、GLM 5.x、Qwen3.8 等模型可在 128GB 内存 Mac 上运行；在 M5 Max 128GB 32K 上下文下实现 34.4 token/s 生成、557 token/s prefill。

🧭 联系你的知识背景 这相当于把模型量化从静态、统一压缩变成面向 MoE 结构的差异化压缩：对不常触达的专家子网络激进降精度，而对影响整体质量的共享路径保持精确，类似于训练时对不同层采用不同学习率来平衡成本与性能。

  • ds4 是 Redis 作者 antirez 开源的本地 LLM 推理引擎，MIT 许可，支持 DeepSeek V4/V4.1、GLM 5.x、Qwen3.8。
  • 采用非对称 2-bit 量化压缩 MoE 路由专家、保持共享路径精确，使模型适配 128GB 内存设备。
  • M5 Max 128GB 32K 上下文下生成 34.4 token/s、prefill 557 token/s；DGX Spark 64K 生成 13.8 token/s。
  • 提供 CLI、HTTP API 和本机 agent，API 兼容 OpenAI/Anthropic 风格，便于本地编码代理接入。

💬 讨论 评论普遍认可 ds4 在 M5 Max 上的实际运行速度和长上下文能力，但关注点分散在跨语言绑定、工具调用表现以及它与其他本地推理工具相比的通用性/专业性权衡。

I've heard some variant of the SaaS doomsday prophecy dozens of times over the past year. Somehow, SaaS is still here and more or less looks the same.

It will probably continue to exist because most businesses are perfectly content to outsource tech problems to someone else for a reasonable fee, and if you think that managing an agent swarm is a seamless replacement for that, I don't know what you tell you. The vast majority of business owners don't want the added complexity in their life.

And for the same reason, most SaaS will probably not migrate to some radically new UX paradigm. Some of it will be vibecoded, but the UX will probably continue to be deterministic. I think this might change once a new breed of "LLM-native" business owners takes charge, but that's going to be a slow process, and it will be hampered by prosaic concerns about interoperability, support, predictable cost, and liability for mistakes (from "the agent did a bit of tax fraud" to "the agent decided to hack my competition").
