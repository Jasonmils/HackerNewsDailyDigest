How to build a diffusion language model

如何构建扩散语言模型

▲ 41 分 · volodia · 3 评论

文章系统拆解了基于掩码扩散构建语言模型的方法：把连续图像中的高斯噪声换成随机掩码，训练一个“生成式 BERT”，再通过迭代去掩码生成整段文本。2024 年起扩散模型首次在质量上对标自回归模型，2026 年已有 Mercury 2 等落地模型，核心价值是并行生成与纠错，可加速推理。

🧭 联系你的知识背景 最需要转换的概念是“噪声”：连续图像用高斯噪声，这里用随机掩码，相当于把你熟悉的 BERT 掩码训练变成可采样的生成过程。

  • 扩散语言模型通过“补全—重掩码”迭代生成整段文本，天然支持并行与纠错。
  • 训练目标等价于随机掩码率的 BERT 损失，证据下界（ELBO）给出严格似然评估。
  • 2024 年出现质量拐点，扩散 LLM 首次在困惑度上接近自回归模型。
  • 块扩散（block diffusion）支持变长生成与键值缓存，更接近生产部署。

💬 讨论 评论共识是扩散语言模型已有实际可用性，尤其 Diffusion Gemma 在 GPU 上输出 token 很快，但仍有优化空间。

Continuous Diffusion Language Models (CDLM's)

连续扩散语言模型（CDLM）

▲ 73 分 · peter_d_sherman · 30 评论

作者回顾连续扩散语言模型从 2021 年兴起、2023 年底几乎被离散扩散完全取代，到近期重新升温的过程，并分析关键原因：ChatGPT 后研究转向性能竞争，且 Plaid-1B 显示连续方法训练效率比自回归低 64 倍。

🧭 联系你的知识背景 扩散模型原用于图像等连续信号，通过加噪后逆向去噪生成；用于语言时需先把离散 token 嵌入连续空间，再应用高斯噪声。

  • 2023 年底后连续扩散语言模型几乎灭绝，新研究几乎全部转向离散扩散
  • Plaid-1B 连续扩散模型训练效率比自回归差 64 倍，这使社区难以认真对待
  • ChatGPT 时刻（2022 年末） 让语言扩散研究从理论优势转向原始性能竞争
  • 连续方法仍有优势，如 token 级不确定性表示 和成熟的采样算法工具箱

💬 讨论 评论区对作者“2020/2021 自回归主导地位尚不稳固”的判断存在分歧，认为解码器自回归当时已主导，且扩散方法更复杂，并未解决生成 token 落入正确分布的核心问题。

> [in 2020/2021] the dominance of autoregression was not as well-established as it is today: GPT-3 had turned some heads, but the ‘ChatGPT moment’ wouldn’t come until late 2022

I disagree with this. Decoders were absolutely dominant in 2020 for chat. GPT2 was considered too dangerous to release, and I remember scrambling to get on the GPT3 waitlist. It worked.

(The only exception I will make is encoder-decoder models which now are often done by decoder-only.)

But what made it go mainstream was RL. RLHF at first, then other improvements like DPO that were less of a pain in the ass to set up. Adding diffusion on top of that would be an even bigger pain in the ass.

Before ChatGPT there really wasn’t much of a concept of pre-training and post-training. It was all pre-training. Post training was what made the bots conversational and not just “continuing the thing you wrote to them”.

So in short, diffusion never took off because it was just a more complicated way to generate tokens, and the real problem was getting tokens in the right distribution.

🔥 回复最多的评论 · simonw · 5 回复

I'm not completely convinced by this comparison between blind chess and prompting LLMs.

In blind chess you get deterministic information about the state of the board: each mental update to your board model can be precise, and you have the full state at every point in time.

LLMs are notoriously non-deterministic, and even at temperature zero you still can't predict exactly where the weights will take you next.

I suppose you can get closer to deterministic if you adopt a prompting style where you almost dictate every line of code, but at that point the coding agent is more of a typing assistant.

The productivity benefits of coding agents unlock themselves when you figure out how to turn short prompts - "add tests that exercise the registration form and check the happy path and all failure states" - into larger changes.

If you're completely blind to the results of those you're going to end up with a system you don't 100% understand very quickly. In blind chess terms you'll no longer know the positions of every piece on the board.
